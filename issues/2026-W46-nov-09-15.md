## 📣 Headlines

**1. Microsoft claims the ASR trifecta with MAI-Transcribe-2.** Microsoft AI
unveiled **MAI-Transcribe-2** (Nov 12), billing it as "the fastest, most
accurate and cheapest speech recognition model in the world." It follows the
MAI-Transcribe-2-Streaming model Microsoft shipped Oct 1 (2.5% WER, 0.13 s
after end-of-speech on AA-WER streaming); the batch sibling's earlier numbers
were 2.0% AA-WER at ~411× real time for $1.67 per 1,000 minutes. The
incumbents just got a serious challenger.
→ [microsoft.ai — MAI-Transcribe-2](https://microsoft.ai/news/mai-transcribe-2-is-the-fastest-most-accurate-and-cheapest-speech-recognition-model-in-the-world/)

**2. ThunderPhone ships Zeus: "Claude Code for AI voice agents."** Instead of
hand-tuning one pipeline, Zeus is an AI agent that builds, tests and tunes your
voice agent — configure the stack, run simulated and live calls, spot issues,
A/B test fixes. Under the hood: many transcription models at once plus audio
piped straight to LLMs, a fast and a slow LLM racing per turn, and speech-to-
speech models fused in for naturalness. ThunderPhone claims the top score on
Big Bench Audio at **99.4%**. The pricing is the quiet headline: calls are
billed **cost-plus** — vendors' prices plus hosting plus a margin that runs
17% at ≤1¢/min and rises gradually to 25% at ≥15¢/min — which treats
model choice as a commodity and moves differentiation up to evals and
orchestration.
→ [thunderphone.com — Introducing ThunderPhone v3, featuring Zeus](https://thunderphone.com/blog/introducing-zeus) (Nov 9)

**3. Zyphra shrinks voice cloning: ZONOS3-Mini.** A compact zero-shot
voice-cloning TTS claiming SOTA performance at small size (Nov 9): a sparse
MoE with **100M active / 800M total parameters**, Apache 2.0, real-time
on-device and in the browser, trained on 112 H100s in 2.5 days. The
quality-per-parameter curve keeps bending.
→ [zyphra.com — ZONOS3-Mini](https://www.zyphra.com/our-work/zonos3-mini)

**4. KugelAudio's Fluid-1: speech-to-speech voice agents at €0.05/min.** A new
S2S model for voice agents — backchannels ("mhm"), mid-call language switching,
8 kHz phone audio, OpenAI Live-compatible API, and any LLM doing the thinking
behind it. On τ²-bench v2 English (150 tasks, self-measured) it scores 84%,
just behind Ava Zero (88%), Phonic 1 (85%) and GPT Live 1 (85%). The S2S field
Phonic-1 topped last week is getting crowded.
→ [kugelaudio.com — Fluid 1](https://www.kugelaudio.com/en/fluid-1)

**5. Helix raises $6.2M pre-seed for on-device voice verification.** Led by
Chicago Ventures (Nov 11), with early partners live: voice biometrics that
verify identity and age on-device, flag synthetic speech, and never send the
voice anywhere — the privacy-preserving answer to a news cycle that keeps
proving why you should verify who is calling.
→ [unite.ai — Helix raises $6.2M](https://www.unite.ai/helix-raises-6-2m-pre-seed-for-on-device-voice-verification/)

**6. Voice-AI healthcare consolidates: Ellipsis Health acquires Kouper.**
Ellipsis Health (voice AI for healthcare management) picked up AI-agent
startup Kouper Health (Nov 11) to broaden scope — a small deal, but vertical
voice AI is starting to roll up.
→ [slator.com — Ellipsis Health acquires Kouper](https://slator.com/ellipsis-health-acquires-kouper/)

**7. Deepfake detectors get a stress test on unseen data.** UC Santa Barbara's
Sound Ethics team benchmarked speech deepfake detectors across **five unseen
datasets** (Nov 13) to measure how well they generalize beyond their training
data. After last week's $129M voice-clone fraud story, this is exactly the
accountability the field needs.
→ [providencejournal.com — UCSB advances speech deepfake detection](https://www.providencejournal.com/press-release/story/145889/sound-ethics-research-team-at-uc-santa-barbara-advances-speech-deepfake-detection/)

## 🔬 Research picks

### TTS & voice generation
- **[Alania-2: Turkish text-to-speech with instant and fine-tuned voice cloning](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13845)** *(Nov 10)* —
488M params (VoxCPM), 1.44% WER on Freya-TR-Eval, first audio at 32 ms; eval
harness and three Turkish datasets released.
- **[RSE-TTS: Compact Text-to-Speech through Shared Text-Aware Reference Encoding and Generation](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15464)** *(Nov 11)* —
another compact-TTS entrant (M5-TTS landed last week); the efficiency race is
not slowing down.
- **[DialInstruct-TTS: Interactive Expressive Speech Generation via Multi-Turn Dialogue with Fine-Grained Temporal Control](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13875)** *(Nov 10)* —
expressive TTS you can steer conversationally, down to the timing.

### ASR & speech understanding
- **[Hy ASR 3.0 Technical Report](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14908)** *(Nov 11)* —
a large-team production ASR tech report (12 pages, project leads listed). The
tech-report-as-launch genre is now a weekly fixture in speech.
- **[Earlybird: Predictive Streaming Speech Recognition in 29M Parameters](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14421)** *(Nov 10)* —
streaming ASR in 29M parameters, model released; the small-but-good ASR race
continues.
- **[Distributionally Robust BEST-RQ For Speech Representation Learning](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14801)** *(Nov 10)* —
IBM Research hardens BEST-RQ pretraining against distribution shift.
- **[Structured Speaker Conditioning for Diarization-Guided Target-Speaker ASR](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16371)** *(Nov 12)* —
"who said what" conditioning, guided by diarization.
- **[Pandemonium: Longform Target-Speaker Speech Recognition with Cross-Speaker Interactions](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10659)** *(Nov 7, just before this window)* —
hour-long "who said what" recognition where multiple speakers actually interact.

### Voice agents & dialogue
- **[An LLM Is All You Need: Data-Efficient Adaptation to Full-Duplex, Word-Level Spoken Dialogue](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14774)** *(Nov 10)* —
full-duplex, word-level spoken dialogue from a plain LLM — no bespoke duplex
architecture required.
- **[Capability-Guided Iterative Online Optimization of Cascaded Spoken-Dialogue Modules](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11322)** *(Nov 8, just before this window)* —
tuning the ASR→LLM→TTS cascade online, per capability, rather than once
offline.
- **[On the information rate of phones, syllables and words](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13990)** *(Nov 10)* —
Herman Kamper asks how much information each unit of speech actually carries.
- **[FEND: Far-End Detection in Outbound Voice AI Calls](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18050)** *(Nov 13)* —
detecting the far end in outbound calls; submitted to EACL 2027.

### Emotional speech
- **[Human-Likeness and Conversational Preference Diverge for Synthesized Agent Speech](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16051)** *(Nov 11)* —
the voice that sounds most human is not the voice people prefer to talk to. A
design consequence for every agent builder.
- **[REAL: Reward Modeling through Evidence-Linked Analysis of Lifelikeness in Generated Speech](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.12406)** *(Nov 9)* —
reward modeling for lifelikeness — RLHF-style signals for how real speech
sounds.

### Deepfake detection & security
- **[Speech Tokenizers are Vulnerable: Transferable Semantic Attack and Robust Tokenizer](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.14929)** *(Nov 11)* —
transferable semantic attacks on speech tokenizers, plus a hardened tokenizer.
If your voice agent speaks in discrete tokens, read this.
- **[The Unseen Threat: How Complementary Combinations of Acoustic Features Compromise Speech Privacy](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16637)** *(Nov 12)* —
innocuous acoustic features, combined, leak more than you'd think.
- **[Task-adaptive anonymisation enables privacy-preserving sharing of clinical speech data](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11273)** *(Nov 8, just before this window)* —
anonymization that lets clinical speech data actually be shared.

### Enhancement & separation
- **[GigaEdge Spench: An SDK for Practical Streaming Speech Enhancement](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13243)** *(Nov 9)* —
streaming speech enhancement shipped as an SDK; enhancement is being
productized.
- **[Virtual-Flow: Virtual Microphone-based Speech Enhancement via Semi-supervised Flow Matching](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11130)** *(Nov 8, just before this window)* —
synthesize the "virtual microphone" you wish you had, with flow matching.
- **[GTCRN-SS: Lightweight Causal Speech Separation with Residual Allocation and Reconstruction Refinement](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09358)** *(Nov 6, just before this window)* —

- **[BAmbiDrop: Array-Agnostic Speech Enhancement with Zero-Shot Binaural Reproduction using Multi-center Ambisonics Encoding](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10561)** *(Nov 7, just before this window)* —
works with whatever mic array it's given; outputs binaural audio.

### Speech & audio in the wild
- **[Cross-Linguistic Acoustic Biomarkers of Cognitive Impairment Across Interactive and Single-Speaker Speech Tasks](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17710)** *(Nov 13)* —
acoustic biomarkers of cognitive impairment across languages and task types.
- **[CallCleaner: Lightweight Bioacoustic Denoising from an Audio Foundation Model](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11491)** *(Nov 8, just before this window)* —

## 📈 One trend to watch

**The voice-agent platform is becoming a product — and this week it started
building itself.** ThunderPhone's Zeus applies the coding-agent loop to voice:
describe the goal, the agent configures the stack, runs simulated and live
calls, and optimizes on your business metrics — with continuous evals
replacing vendor marketing as the source of truth, and cost-plus pricing that
treats the underlying models as commodities. Around it, the open-source world
is cloning the platform layer (self-hosted agents, local dictation), and arXiv
is supplying the hard parts: turn-taking prediction with human benchmarks
(AVCC), full-duplex adaptation of plain LLMs, and the streaming-enhancement
SDKs the platforms lean on. If calls get priced like infrastructure, watch
where the margin moves: to evals, orchestration, and whoever owns the
customer's definition of "it worked."
