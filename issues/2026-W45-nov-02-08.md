## 📣 Headlines

**1. OpenAI ships a new voice stack in the API.** Three models landed the week of
Nov 5: **GPT-Realtime-2** (their first voice model with GPT-5-class reasoning, built
to "handle harder requests and carry the conversation forward naturally"),
**GPT-Realtime-Translate** (live speech-to-speech translation, 70+ input languages
to 13 output languages, keeping pace with the speaker), and **GPT-Realtime-Whisper**
(streaming speech-to-text that transcribes as the speaker talks). A playable demo
of Realtime-2 is on the announcement page.
→ [openai.com — Advancing voice intelligence with new models in the API](https://openai.com/index/advancing-voice-intelligence-with-new-models-in-the-api/)

**2. Elvis and Eminem rights holders sue Suno — the ELVIS Act gets its first big
test.** Elvis Presley rightsholders sued the AI music company for **more than
$20M**, alleging its model imitates the King's voice; **Eight Mile Style**
(Eminem's publisher) filed a separate **$47.7M** suit covering 269 songs (Nov 7–8).
The cases will turn on Tennessee's 2024 ELVIS Act, which protects voice likeness —
the first law of its kind.
→ [Music Business Worldwide](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/elvis-presleys-rightsholders-and-other-song-owners-sue-suno-for-more-than-202m-alleging-its-ai-imitates-the-kings-voice-eight-mile-style-has-filed-a-separate-47-7m-suit-over-269/)

**3. DOJ charges three men in a $129M voice-cloning fraud.** Federal prosecutors
charged three men (Nov 8) in a scheme that allegedly stole **more than $129 million**
from hospitals and banks using AI voice clones of "the boss" — finance chiefs and
company leaders phoning subordinates with urgent transfer requests. The NYT's
account is the fullest; every incident-response team should read it.
→ [The New York Times — "When the Boss on the Phone Isn't Real"](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/08/technology/ai-voice-fraud-129-million.html)

**4. Phonic-1 tops speech-to-speech rankings — and raised $27.5M.** The S2S startup
announced **Phonic-1** with its **$27.5M Series A led by First Round** (announcement
Oct 29; trade coverage landed this week). Claims worth noting: #1 on the Artificial
Analysis Speech-to-Speech Index, **600 ms** round-trip latency, and the ability to
**call tools while the caller is still talking** — including a demo 44-minute call
with 123 tool calls. 50+ languages.
→ [phonic.ai — Introducing Phonic-1 and our $27.5M Series A](https://phonic.ai/blog/phonic-1)

**5. Meta gives its Muse agent a voice.** Muse Voice rolled out (Nov 6): real-time
voice conversations with the Muse AI agent, hundreds of selectable voices, and
expressive avatars — a consumer-grade play that puts a voice-first assistant in
front of Meta's user base.
→ [Gadgets 360](https://www.gadgets360.com/ai/news/meta-muse-ai-voice-chat-feature-expressive-avatars-launched) · [News18](https://www.news18.com/tech/meta-muse-ai-gets-a-face-to-face-voice-chat-feature-with-hundreds-of-voices/)

**6. Google teaches Gemini TTS to remix voices by prompt.** **Voice remixing** in
`gemini-3.8-flash-tts` (docs live, surfaced on HN Nov 4): hand it a `base_voice`
plus a natural-language instruction — shift age, pitch, energy, timbre, accent —
and `POST /v1beta/voices` returns a new stored `voice_...` ID usable across Flash
and Flash-Lite TTS. Voice editing as an API primitive.
→ [Google AI Studio docs — Voice remixing](https://aistudio.google.com/docs/voice-remixing)

**7. Hume launches a "decision model" for audio.** Ask questions of raw audio — no
transcript — and get structured answers per 5-second window: naturalness, empathy,
timing, escalation-worthiness. Hume reports **70.7% agreement with a human crowd vs
56.5% for a single human rater**, across 1B+ client hours processed. Quietly a big
deal for call-center QA.
→ [hume.ai — Decision Model API](https://www.hume.ai/products/decision-model-api)

## 🔬 Research picks

### TTS & voice generation

- **[TTSonic: A Unified Framework for Composable and Controllable Speech Generation](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04282)** *(Nov 3)* —
  One speech foundation model for zero-shot TTS, voice cloning, prosody cloning, and
  instruction-following. The "unified composability" framing is the interesting part.
- **[M5-TTS: A Mini-Scale Multilingual TTS LM with Multi-Token Prediction](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10162)** *(Nov 6)* —
  50M parameters (excluding codec), multilingual + Chinese multi-dialect, zero-shot
  cloning, **CPU streaming**. GRPO post-training. A serious entry in the
  small-but-good TTS race.
- **[Guidance at Pure Noise Erodes Speaker Similarity in Few-Step Flow-Matching TTS](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11179)** *(Nov 8)* —
  Why your 2–4-step Euler decoder loses 1–6 points of speaker SIM vs the official
  10-step recipe: CFG at the first (pure-noise) step is the culprit. Practical fix
  inside; matters to everyone distilling flow-matching TTS.
- **[Ruby-OPD: Pronunciation Controllability via On-Policy Distillation](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06403)** *(Nov 4)* —
  Japanese TTS with fine pronunciation control without discarding lexical
  semantics — on-policy distillation into flow-matching TTS.
- **[Detector-Guided Vocoder Training for Adversarial TTS](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04511)** *(Nov 3)* —
  Fine-tunes the vocoder *with the deepfake detector in the training loop* to
  defeat detection. The arms race, formalized.
- **[Ninada: Sub-Clause Split-Graph VITS Streaming](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10035)** *(Nov 5)* —
  Client-side (browser/mobile) TTS across 12 Indic scripts: sub-clause streaming
  cuts time-to-first-audio and shrinks checkpoints from 138.8 MB float32.

### ASR & speech understanding

- **[Confucius4-R2T2: A Stable-Prefix Streaming ASR Model](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06043)** *(Nov 4)* —
  "Real real-time" transcription with stable prefixes — aimed at the latency-vs-
  stability tradeoff that streaming ASR actually lives and dies by.
- **[KeyASRBench: Measuring Whole-Phrase Keyword Preservation in Real-World ASR](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10852)** *(Nov 7)* —
  WER hides the failures that hurt: product names, IDs, dosages. This benchmark
  scores whole-phrase keyword preservation instead.
- **[Decode Before You Hear: Speculative Listening for Full-Duplex Speech LMs](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06982)** *(Nov 4)* —
  Speculative decoding adapted to chunk-interleaved full-duplex speech models —
  respond before the speaker finishes, cheaply.
- **[Pandemonium: Longform Target-Speaker ASR with Cross-Speaker Interactions](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10659)** *(Nov 7)* —
  Hour-long "who said what" recognition where multiple speakers actually interact.
- **[E-Gaborformer: A Strictly Causal Speech Recognizer](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08092)** *(Nov 5)* —
  Gabor-dictionary convolutions + delta-rule memory for streaming, strictly causal
  ASR.
- **[Text-Only Domain Adaptation for Low-Resource Medical ASR (Hausa)](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05744)** *(Nov 3)* —
  Shallow fusion + density-ratio rescoring, no in-domain audio. Cheap wins for
  low-resource domains.

### Voice agents & dialogue

- **[Benchmarking Speaker Embedders in the Sub-Second Regime for Real-Time Voice AI Agents](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09516)** *(Nov 6)* —
  Which speaker embeddings work when you only have <1s of audio? Directly relevant
  to agent pipelines that must personalize early.
- **[Real-Time Short-Response Selection for Spoken Dialogue Systems](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09057)** *(Nov 6)* —
  Selecting the response before generation finishes — a take on turn-taking beyond
  "just stream tokens faster".
- **[SciPhi-Chat: Training a Spatial Audio Agent](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04114)** *(Nov 3)* —
  QA, dialogue, and tool use grounded in spatial audio — agents that know *where*
  sounds come from.
- **[Capability-Guided Iterative Online Optimization of Cascaded Spoken-Dialogue Modules](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11322)** *(Nov 8)* —
  Tuning the ASR→LLM→TTS cascade online, per capability, rather than once offline.

### Emotional speech

- **[Mind the Divergence: Reliability-Aware Fusion for Conversational Emotion Recognition](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08860)** *(Nov 6)* —
  Uses divergence between modality predictions to gate fusion and adaptively prompt
  an LLM — a cleaner answer to "how do I trust my audio features today?"
- **[Fuzzy-FedAvg: Federated Speech Emotion Recognition](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08818)** *(Nov 6)* —
  Server-aware client selection for SER across heterogeneous devices.
- **[Concordance-Optimal Continuous Music Emotion Estimation](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08966)** *(Nov 6)* —
  Continuous valence/arousal with variance decomposition and conformal prediction
  intervals — uncertainty-aware emotion tracking.

### Deepfake detection & security

- **[When Normality Shifts: Multi-Level WavLM Modeling for Open-World Deepfake Detection](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11465)** *(Nov 8)* —
  Attack-free detection: model *bona fide* speech, score deviations from normality.
  Generalizes to unseen generators — exactly what the DOJ case above demands.
- **[Conflict-Aware Meta-Learning for Generalizable Speech Deepfake Detection](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08758)** *(Nov 5)* —
  Complementary take on the same generalization problem.
- **[VIPBench: A Human-Aligned Benchmark for Voice Identity Perception in the Age of Voice Cloning](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05638)** *(Nov 3)* —
  Can models tell whose voice it is — and whose clone? Benchmarks voice-identity
  perception against human judgments.

### Enhancement & separation

- **[Virtual-Flow: Virtual Microphone Speech Enhancement via Semi-supervised Flow Matching](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11130)** *(Nov 8)* —
  Synthesize the "virtual microphone" you wish you had, with flow matching.
- **[BAmbiDrop: Array-Agnostic Speech Enhancement with Zero-Shot Binaural Reproduction](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.10561)** *(Nov 7)* —
  Works with whatever mic array it's given; outputs binaural audio.
- **[AISHELL8-FISHEYE: Audio-Visual Dataset for Target Speaker Extraction](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09368)** *(Nov 6)* —
  Fisheye video + audio for target-speaker extraction, with distortion-aware
  baselines.
- **[SubRoformer: Subband Time-Domain Music Source Separation](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08130)** *(Nov 5)* —
  Music rather than speech, but the subband TD architecture travels.

### Speech & audio in the wild

- **[DIY Customized Speech Recognition for a Person with Speech Disabilities](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11327)** *(Nov 8)* —
  A usable pipeline for personalizing ASR to one dysarthric speaker. Assistive
  speech tech moving from paper to practice.
- **[From Sound to Occupancy: Privacy-Aware Audio Sensing for Buildings](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11564)** *(Nov 8)* —
  Occupancy from sound, designed to not leak speech content.
- **[CallCleaner: Lightweight Bioacoustic Denoising from an Audio Foundation Model](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11491)** *(Nov 8)* —
  Animal-vocalization denoising without clean training pairs.

- **[LAION-Audio-XL](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11592)** *(Nov 8)* — **1.48 billion**
  audio samples from academic, web, Common Crawl, and YouTube sources, with scaling
  studies for contrastive language-audio pretraining. The headline dataset of the week.
- **[VoxCanvas-41M](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08251)** *(Nov 5)* — large-scale bilingual
  dataset for *instruction-based speech editing*, plus learning-dynamics analysis

- **[LibriSpeech-Real-QA](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06496)** *(Nov 4)* — streaming
  question answering over speech: answers must arrive while the question is still
  being asked.
- **[GlobalTap](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11137)** *(Nov 8)* — globally diverse beat
  tracking via crowdsourced tapping.

  the Telefónica TID ASR system report is a useful read on adapting ASR to
  low-resource, real-world conditions ([arXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.11497)).

## 📈 One trend to watch

**Speech-to-speech is eating the cascade.** Three separate items this week —
Phonic-1 (S2S with tool-calling at 600 ms), Rime's fine-tunable S2S for enterprise,
and OpenAI's Realtime-2 — all position end-to-end speech-to-speech as the
architecture for voice agents, not a research curiosity. Meanwhile the research
pile pushes the same direction from below: speculative listening for full-duplex
models, sub-second speaker embedding, short-response selection. The
ASR→LLM→TTS pipeline isn't dead — see KeyASRBench and Ninada — but the frontier
is consolidating around models that hear tone and hesitate like a person, not a
transcript. Watch whether the deepfake-detection side (MLN-WavLM, VIPBench) can
keep pace; the $129M fraud charges suggest it has to.
