# Speech Weekly

A weekly digest of what matters in speech and audio AI: research, news, and product
announcements — curated by hand, published every week.

**Focus areas:** text-to-speech (TTS) · automatic speech recognition (ASR) · voice
cloning · voice agents · emotional speech understanding & generation · speech
datasets · voice activity detection · speech separation & enhancement · deepfake
detection · spoken dialogue systems.

## How to read it

Each week's issue lives in [`issues/`](issues/) as a dated markdown file and is
mirrored to a GitHub Issue labeled `weekly`.

- **Headlines** — the stories that would matter to someone building in this space.
- **Research** — arXiv picks across all focus areas, with one-line takeaways.
- **Open source** — new repos worth a look, with stars at press time.
- **Data & benchmarks** — datasets, evaluation suites, challenges.
- **One trend to watch** — what this week's pile of items says about where things go.

## Publishing cadence

| Week covered | Issue file | Published |
|--------------|-----------|-----------|
| Nov 2 – 8, 2026 | [`issues/2026-W45-nov-02-08.md`](issues/2026-W45-nov-02-08.md) | Nov 11, 2026 |

New issues land every Wednesday, covering Monday–Sunday of the previous week.

## Submissions

PRs welcome: add an item to the *next* issue draft (`issues/next.md`) with a link
and a one-line summary of why it matters. Self-promotion is fine if it's honest.
